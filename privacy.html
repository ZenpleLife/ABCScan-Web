Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ABCScan. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our application and website.

2. Data Collection & Usage

Local Processing: ABCScan is designed with privacy at its core. All scanning and image processing are performed directly on your device. We do not upload your scanned documents, images, or extracted text to our servers.

No Personal Data: We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or location unless you voluntarily provide it (e.g., when contacting support).

3. Third-Party Services

We do not share your data with third parties. Our application does not include third-party trackers or analytics that collect your personal information.

4. Security

Since your data stays on your device, its security depends on the security of your device. We recommend using standard device security features (passcodes, biometrics) to protect your scans.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@zenplelife.com.
